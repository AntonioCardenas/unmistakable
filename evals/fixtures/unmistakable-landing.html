How the burr carrier holds two microns

The Helvetia Mill 83 keeps runout under 2 microns because the burr carrier and motor shaft seat are line-bored in a single clamping on a 5-axis mill. Nothing is re-fixtured between operations, so the two burr faces stay parallel to within the tolerance of the machine itself.

Lower runout narrows the particle distribution, which is what lets an 83 mm flat burr pull clear, separated flavors from light-roast espresso.

Does the Helvetia Mill 83 work for both espresso and filter?

Yes. The stepless collar moves the burr gap from 0.05 mm to 1.4 mm, which covers Turkish through coarse filter. The burr geometry is tuned for espresso clarity first.

How is the two-micron runout verified?

Each assembled burr carrier is measured at four points on a dial indicator rig. The readings ship on a printed report matched to your serial number.
