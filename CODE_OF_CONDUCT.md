# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards

- Focus constructively on technical craft, accessibility, and clear evidence.
- Respect differing aesthetic contexts and project constraints.
- Credit upstream research and open-source contributors accurately.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a confidential security advisory or contacting the repository maintainer listed in `package.json`.
